During my internship at **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)**, I actively participated in professional events organized by the FCAJ community and AWS. Each event provided new knowledge about Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and modern technology trends, while also giving me opportunities to interact with experts, learn from practical experiences, and broaden my career orientation in the technology field.

Through these events, I not only kept up to date with new technologies but also gained a better understanding of how businesses apply AI and AWS in real-world scenarios. These experiences provided valuable knowledge that I could apply to my studies and personal projects.

## [Event 1](4.1-Event1)

- **Event Name:** FCAJ Community Day - June 2026
- **Time:** 09:00, **June 27, 2026**
- **Location:** **26th Floor, Bitexco Building, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City**
- **Role:** Participant

The event focused on sharing the latest trends in AI and Cloud Computing through presentations by experts from AWS and technology companies. The program covered various topics, including Voice AI, DevOps Agent, Amazon Q, MCP Server, and practical experiences in building and operating AI systems in enterprise environments.

Through this event, I gained a better understanding of Voice AI architecture, how AI can be applied to DevOps, human resource management, and enterprise data security. I also learned about a product development mindset based on real customer needs and the role of AI in the digital transformation process.

## [Event 2](4.2Event2)

- **Event Name:** FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week Showcase
- **Time:** 09:00, **July 25, 2026**
- **Location:** **26th Floor, Bitexco Building, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City**
- **Role:** Participant

This event was the showcase and closing session of the Agentic AI Build Week program, where participating teams presented the Agentic AI products they had developed throughout the Build Week. The teams shared their ideas, system architectures, development processes, and practical experiences in building projects on the AWS platform.

Through this event, I was introduced to various Agentic AI solutions applied to areas such as e-commerce, data analysis, Cloud infrastructure design, security monitoring, and Fintech. In addition to technical knowledge, I learned how to identify real-world business problems, develop an MVP, collaborate within a team, and present a product effectively. These are valuable experiences that can be applied to academic projects as well as my future career development.

## [Event 3](4.3-Event3)

- **Event Name:** AgentForge Workshop – Day 2: Personalization, Evaluation & Optimization
- **Time:** 09:00 – 11:00, **August 8, 2026**
- **Location:** **26th Floor, Bitexco Building, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City**
- **Role:** Participant

AgentForge Workshop – Day 2 was an in-depth workshop on **Advanced Amazon Bedrock AgentCore**, focusing on building AI Agents with personalization, performance evaluation, and operational optimization capabilities on the AWS platform. The program combined knowledge-sharing sessions with **Hands-on Labs**, allowing participants to directly explore key features of Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, including Memory, Agent Observability, AgentCore Evaluations, and Harness.

Through the workshop, I learned how to build AI Agents capable of maintaining user context, evaluate Agent performance, and optimize their effectiveness using tools provided by AWS. In addition, the hands-on activities helped me gain a better understanding of the development and deployment process for AI Agents on the Amazon Bedrock platform, while improving my skills in working with AWS Generative AI services for future real-world projects.
