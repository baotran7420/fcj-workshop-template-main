## Reflection & Personal Contributions

During the development and deployment of the **AI Learning Assistant Platform** on **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**, the team had the opportunity to apply Cloud Computing knowledge to a practical system.

Throughout the deployment process, the team gained practical experience in selecting and configuring AWS resources, deploying applications using Docker, setting up CI/CD, monitoring the system, and managing costs.

In addition to the achievements, the implementation process also involved several challenges and issues that needed to be addressed. At the same time, the team identified potential improvements to enhance the scalability, stability, and operational capabilities of the system in the future.

The Reflection section is divided into two main parts:

### [1. Challenges](./5.6.1-challenges-encountered)
   - Issues encountered during the deployment process.
   - The causes of these issues and how they were addressed.
   - Lessons learned from the implementation process.

### [2. Future Enhancements](./5.6.2-lessons-learned-future-improvements)
   - Potential improvements to the system architecture.
   - Scalability and high-availability improvements.
   - Future directions for evolving the system from an MVP to a Production environment.
