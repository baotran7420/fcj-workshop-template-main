Event 1

Summary Report: “FCAJ Community Day - June 2026”

Event Objectives

FCAJ Community Day - June 2026 is a community event organized by FCAJ (AWS Study Group) to highlight the latest trends in Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence (AI). Through talks from experts and companies, the event helps participants learn from real-world system implementation experiences and introduces modern AI technologies used across many industries.

The main goals of the event are:

  • Share practical experience in building and operating Cloud and AI systems.
  • Introduce modern AI trends such as Voice AI, AI Agent, and Amazon Q.
  • Update participants on AI applications in DevOps, human resources, and enterprise security.
  • Create opportunities for networking and learning among the AWS community, students, and industry experts.

Speakers

  • Steve Tran (Cloud Thinker): Shared his startup journey and practical experience in building products and operating real systems.
  • Nghi (Renova Cloud),Trung (CEO of Revve AI), and Kiet (Voice AI real-world demo): Presented on the Voice AI architecture, system components, and implementation challenges in Vietnam.
  • Bao and Nguyen (Cloud Kinetis): Introduced DevOps Agent and how AI can automate operations, monitoring, and incident handling.
  • Truong and Minh Anh (Noventic): Shared how Amazon Q can be used in HR to improve recruitment and workforce management.
  • Toan Nguyen (AWS Security Builder) and Nghi (Renova Cloud): Discussed data security and how to integrate Amazon Q with enterprise systems through an MCP Server.

Key Highlights

1. Sharing from Cloud Thinker

The event opened with Steve Tran sharing his startup journey and practical lessons from developing technology products.

Key points included:

  • An idea only becomes valuable when it is turned into a real product.
  • Execution is the decisive factor for startup success.
  • It is important to identify the right Customer Champion to understand customer needs.
  • Technology should not be built just because it is trendy, but should solve a real business problem.
  • Products should be tested early to gather feedback and improve continuously.

This talk helped participants understand that technology is only a tool, while the core value lies in solving the customer’s real problem.

2. Voice AI - The Future of Communication

Speakers from Renova Cloud, Revve AI is Nghi, Trung, Kiet introduced the overall architecture of a modern Voice AI system.

The architecture consists of three main components:

  • Speech-to-Text (STT): Converts speech into text.
  • Large Language Model (LLM): Analyzes context and generates a response.
  • Text-to-Speech (TTS): Converts the response back into speech.

The speakers also introduced the Speech-to-Speech model and compared the advantages and disadvantages of both architectures.

Some challenges when deploying Voice AI in Vietnam include:

  • Vietnamese has limited training data.
  • It is difficult to handle multiple regional accents and speaking styles.
  • Speaker gender detection is needed to generate natural responses.
  • Barge-in support is required during conversations.
  • Latency must be minimized to deliver real-time communication.

From this session, I gained a clearer understanding of how to build a complete conversational AI system and the practical challenges of deploying it in real environments.

3. DevOps Agent

Bao and Nguyen introduced DevOps Agent, an AI-based solution that supports operations teams.

Notable functions include:

  • Collecting and aggregating logs from multiple sources.
  • Performing root cause analysis of incidents.
  • Providing remediation suggestions based on historical data.
  • Automatically generating incident reports.
  • Helping DevOps engineers reduce mean time to resolution (MTTR).

Through real examples, the speakers showed that AI does not replace DevOps engineers but acts as an intelligent assistant that reduces workload and improves operational efficiency.

4. Amazon Q in Human Resources

Truong and Minh Anh shared how businesses can use Amazon Q to optimize recruitment and HR operations.

Amazon Q can support:

  • Screening candidate profiles.
  • Analyzing qualifications and experience.
  • Summarizing information for interviews.
  • Answering internal questions.
  • Automating administrative tasks.

Using AI helps reduce processing time, improve recruitment quality, and increase HR productivity.

5. Security and Amazon Q Integration with MCP Server

The final session focused on data security when deploying AI in enterprises.

The speakers introduced the MCP (Model Context Protocol) Server as a solution for connecting Amazon Q with internal data sources.

Key benefits include:

  • Allowing AI to access enterprise data securely.
  • Avoiding exposure of data to the public internet.
  • Ensuring privacy and information security.
  • Making integration with existing systems easier.

This part helped me understand that data security must always be a top priority when deploying AI in enterprises.
